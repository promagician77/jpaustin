      Buyers meet Libby before they meet you

      This is not a question about your product. It is a question about what your website answers, and right now it answers nothing.

      To be clear

      I am not suggesting anything about your material. I am pointing out that your site makes a safety claim with nothing behind it, in the one mineral where buyers have learned to ask.

      The situationWhy it reaches you
      Vermiculite from Libby, Montana carried tremolite asbestos Mined by W.R. Grace until 1990 and shipped to more than 200 processing plants across the United States, much of it sold as Zonolite attic insulation.
      It is still live news The EPA cleanup has passed $600 million and continues. Libby was in the national press again this year.
      The rule buyers apply Material from an unverified mine source gets treated as potentially asbestos-containing. Not because it is, but because nobody has said otherwise.
      Where that leaves you Trading since 1962, through the whole of that period, with a website that does not name a mine, publish an SDS or show a test result. The buyer fills in the silence themselves.
      1
      Name the source

      One sentence saying where your ore comes from. For a company that has been doing this since 1962 and buys from a known mine, this is the single highest-value sentence on the whole site.

      2
      Publish the safety data sheet

      A PDF on every product page. You almost certainly already have one, because your industrial customers will have asked for it. It just is not on the website.

      3
      Put the OMRI certificate where growers can download it

      You have the certification. Right now it is a sentence. As a downloadable certificate it is a reason to buy from you instead of the next supplier.

      4
      Say it plainly, once, and stop

      A short, factual sourcing and safety page. Not defensive, not long. Suppliers who address this directly look more credible than suppliers who never raise it.

      The upside

      Almost nobody in this trade does this well. A clear sourcing statement and a downloadable SDS would put you ahead of most of your competition on the thing buyers are quietly worried about, and it costs you a page and a few PDFs you already own.
